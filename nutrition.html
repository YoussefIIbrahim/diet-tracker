Starting targets for the first three weeks. Review weight trend, waist and gym performance before adjusting.

Morning · 350–450 kcal

Breakfast

25–40 g protein.

  • Eggs + light cheese + protein toast + vegetables
  • Eggs + cottage cheese + toast
  • 250–400 g potato omelette
  • 40–60 g muesli + milk + 150–250 g skyr + berries
  • 180–280 g fava + eggs + 50–80 g pita
Office · 100–200 kcal

Morning snack

Only if hungry.

  • Berries 150–250 g
  • Grapes or mango 150–200 g
  • Watermelon 300–600 g
  • Skyr 150–250 g
  • Protein pudding 150–200 g
12:00–13:30 · 500–600 kcal

Lunch

Huge salad or standard plate.

  • 400–700 g vegetables
  • 140–200 g chicken, 160–220 g shrimp, 130–180 g beef, or 120–170 g salmon
  • Optional pita 50–80 g, potato 200–300 g, or couscous 40–70 g dry
  • Measured feta, avocado, chickpeas or yogurt dressing
16:00–17:00 · 300–450 kcal

Anti-binge meal

20–35 g protein. Eat before commuting.

  • Protein wrap + 80–120 g chicken + light cheese
  • Protein toast + lean cold cuts + cheese
  • 250–350 g skyr + berries + 20–40 g muesli
  • 200–250 g cottage cheese + pita/toast
  • Leftover protein + 200–300 g potato
Evening · 650–750 kcal

Dinner

Make the plate huge.

  • Protein 150–220 g cooked
  • 60–90 g dry rice/pasta/couscous, 300–500 g potato, pita or wraps
  • Vegetables 300–600+ g
  • Oil usually 5–10 g, or avocado/cheese/fattier protein
  • Wrap night, burger + wedges, loaded potato, chicken pasta
22:00 · 100–250 kcal

Late snack

Reserve it intentionally.

  • Skyr, quark, cottage cheese or protein pudding
  • Berries 150–250 g or watermelon 300–500 g
  • Grapes 100–150 g
  • 1–2 light Babybel

Green / Yellow / Red

Color describes life friction, not permission to abandon tracking. Yellow simplifies food. Red may reach 2,500–2,600 kcal if it prevents a genuine binge.

Takeaway is food

Start with one planned takeaway weekly. Pre-decide KFC. Try one substantial burger + fries, or one shawarma + fries + measured toum + salad.

After overeating

No fasting, punishment cardio or “restart Monday.” The next meal is Home Mode. Leftovers can be tomorrow’s food.

Travel Mode

No scale required. Three meals, protein each time, fruit/veg often, one indulgent component. Home Mode resumes with the first meal home.

Tracking convention

Rice, pasta and couscous use dry weight. Meat and fish use cooked weight. Measure oils, sauces, cheese and nuts.

Progress

Daily weight but judge the 7-day average. Waist weekly. Photos every four weeks. Aim roughly 0.4–0.7 kg/week.
